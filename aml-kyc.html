AML & KYC Policy

Rogers Investment is committed to maintaining a secure platform and supporting efforts to prevent financial crime through appropriate identity verification and compliance practices.

1. Purpose

This Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) Policy outlines our general approach to identity verification, fraud prevention, and platform security.

2. Identity Verification

Where applicable, users may be requested to verify their identity before certain services or account activities are completed.

Verification requests may include documents or information reasonably necessary to confirm a user's identity.

3. Information That May Be Requested

  • Full legal name
  • Date of birth
  • Residential address
  • Government-issued identification
  • Proof of address where applicable
  • Additional verification information if required

4. Monitoring & Compliance

We may monitor account activity to help identify unusual, suspicious, or potentially fraudulent behavior. Where appropriate, additional verification may be requested before certain activities are processed.

5. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for providing accurate and up-to-date information. Submitting false, misleading, or fraudulent information may result in account restrictions or other appropriate action.

6. Data Protection

Information collected for verification purposes is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and appropriate security measures designed to protect user data.

7. Policy Updates

This AML & KYC Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our services, operational practices, or applicable legal requirements.

8. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this policy, please contact our support team.
