This Cookie Policy explains how Rogers Investment uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, enhance website performance, and understand how visitors interact with our platform.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember user preferences, improve functionality, and provide a better browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We may use cookies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Maintain website functionality.
  • Improve website performance.
  • Analyze visitor traffic and usage trends.
  • Enhance website security.

3. Types of Cookies

  • Essential Cookies – Required for core website functionality.
  • Performance Cookies – Help us understand website performance.
  • Analytics Cookies – Provide anonymous visitor statistics.
  • Preference Cookies – Remember user settings and preferences.

4. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality and user experience.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services integrated into our website may place cookies on your device. These services operate under their own privacy and cookie policies.
